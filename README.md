#

I am a passionate **Data Analyst and Finance Professional** with over 6 years of experience across financial accounting, financial analysis, and data-driven projects. I enjoy transforming complex data into meaningful insights that support better decisions and solve real-world problems.

My technical expertise includes **Microsoft Excel, SQL, Power BI, Python, Tableau, and Data Studio**, with experience in data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, statistical analysis, data visualization, financial modelling, and business intelligence. My interests also extend to **Financial Engineering, Quantitative Finance, Equity Research, and Data Analytics**.

I have worked on **research projects, project proposals, research methodologies, reports, assessments, proofreading, editing, and PowerPoint presentations**. I enjoy combining strong research skills with analytical thinking to communicate complex ideas clearly.

I am continuously learning, building practical projects, and expanding my knowledge across **Data, Finance, and Technology**. I believe that good analysis is not just about numbers—it is about asking the right questions, uncovering meaningful insights, and using them to create impact.

I enjoy connecting with like-minded professionals, collaborating on meaningful projects, sharing knowledge, and learning from others. This GitHub portfolio is a collection of my projects, experiments, and continuous growth as an **Analyst and Finance Professional**.
